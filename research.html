Research

My research focuses on multimodal AI for scientific discovery and applications, developing models that reason across images, graphs, signals, sequences, and text. I am interested in reliable AI for scientific discovery, with additional interests in frugal AI and model efficiency.

Multimodal AI

Developing models with multimodal representation learning methods that align information across heterogeneous data sources. My work on cross-modal alignment enables models to jointly reason over data such as : images, sequences, text, and graphs.

  • Vision and Language models pre-training
  • Modality Alignment
  • Uncertainty quantification and calibration
  • Scientific discovery
VLMs Multimodal Learning Contrastive Learning Cross-modal Alignment

Biomedical AI

AI methods to biomedical challenges, including protein function prediction, protein-protein interaction analysis, and medical image understanding. My research leverages biological data, sequence encoders, and large language models to advance scientific discovery.

  • Multimodal biomolecular structure representation and understanding
  • Structure-aware modelling of biomolecular interactions
  • Generative design and property prediction for biomolecules
Medical Imaging Computational Biology Proteomics Drug Design

Computer Vision

Developing and improving AI methods for image and video understanding, with a focus on scientific and engineering applications. Exploring visual representation learning, spatiotemporal modelling, and generative approaches such as diffusion models for analysis, prediction, and discovery.

  • Image and video understanding
  • Spatiotemporal modelling and motion analysis
  • Visual representation learning
  • Diffusion and generative models
  • Scientific and engineering vision applications
Computer Vision Video Understanding Spatiotemporal AI Diffusion Models Scientific AI

Responsible & Frugal Generative AI

I am interested in developing reliable, controllable, and resource-efficient generative AI methods for scientific discovery, exploring how these models can be made more trustworthy, data-efficient, and computationally sustainable.

  • Reliable and controllable LLMs for science
  • Scientific generation and multimodal captioning
  • Human-in-the-loop synthetic data generation and validation
  • Gen AI designed around limited compute/data/energy
