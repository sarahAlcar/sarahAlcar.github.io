Ongoing Research Projects

Protein & Molecular Sequence/Structure-to-Text Generation

2025 -

Researcher — DASciM Lab, École Polytechnique

Development of multimodal generative models for translating protein sequences, 3D structures, and protein-protein interactions into natural language descriptions. The work includes Prot2Text-V2 for protein function captioning, as well as PPI2Text for structure-aware protein interaction captioning, combining structural representations, multimodal alignment, and language modeling.

Protein AI Molecular AI Multimodal Learning LLMs

Cultural Evaluation & Alignment of Large Language Models

2025 -

Researcher — DASciM Lab, École Polytechnique

Research on evaluating and understanding the cultural biases, values, and behavioral differences encoded by large language models. Contributed to research on cultural evaluation and culturally grounded model behavior through CARTE, Masked Advantage, and Every Answer Counts, investigating how LLMs respond to culturally diverse contexts and how their evaluations can be made more representative and reliable across cultures.
